# v0.5.1 · Softer creatures, cleaner edges 🐟

This update refines the artwork and motion of the marine expansion introduced in v0.5.0.

## What's improved

- Redrew all 60 expansion creatures with rounder shapes and softer toy-like materials to better match the original collection.
- Replaced the three white-background expansion atlases with transparent artwork. Cleanup now runs before downsampling, preserves pale markings and fine antenna tips, and removes neighboring atlas fragments.
- Gave jellyfish a gentle up-and-down pulse with bell contraction and trailing tentacles. Shells stay still; shrimp, crabs, turtles and seals use separate, restrained appendage motions.
- Added transparency regression tests and light/dark artwork checks, plus motion checks for WebGL and the software fallback.

The collection still contains **160 creatures, including 10 legends, and 640 collectible appearances**. Existing collections and settings remain compatible.

## Download & play

Download **thank-you-for-the-fish-macos-arm64.zip**, unzip it, and move **Thank You for the Fish.app** into Applications. Quit the previous version before replacing it.

This packaged build is for **Apple Silicon / M-series Macs**. It is not Developer ID signed or notarized. SHA-256 checksums are included in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.
