# Changelog / 版本记录

## [0.5.1](https://github.com/AmethystineAlpaca/thank-you-for-the-fish/releases/tag/v0.5.1)

- Refreshed all 60 expansion creatures with softer, rounder artwork.
- Cleaned transparent sprite edges while preserving pale details and antenna tips.
- Refined jellyfish pulses and anatomy-specific motion; shells remain still.
- Added transparency regression coverage and WebGL / Canvas motion checks.

重绘 60 种扩展生物，改善透明边缘、细触须与按身体结构区分的动画。兼容原有收藏和设置。详见 [双语发布说明](docs/releases/v0.5.1.md)。

## [0.5.0](https://github.com/AmethystineAlpaca/thank-you-for-the-fish/releases/tag/v0.5.0)

- Expanded from 100 to 160 creatures, with 10 legends in a sixth rarity tier.
- Added legendary badges and filtering; all four traits are available for legends.
- Added configurable fishing rounds and automatic rest with a reclining angler.
- Improved creature motion and collection display.

新增 60 种生物，包含 10 种传说；收藏扩展至 640 种外观。加入可设置的垂钓轮次与自动休息。

## [0.4.3](https://github.com/AmethystineAlpaca/thank-you-for-the-fish/releases/tag/v0.4.3)

- First public Apple Silicon release: a draggable, resizable desktop boat.
- 100 creatures, four traits, local collections and an animated field guide.
- Configurable bite intervals, preview catches, and offline play after installation.

首个公开版本：桌面小船、100 种生物、四种性状、本地收藏与可调整的垂钓间隔。
